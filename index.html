        How to play

        1. Everyone gets 2 private cards. 5 shared cards land in the middle: 3, then 1, then 1.
        2. Make the best 5-card hand from your 2 cards plus the 5 shared ones.
        3. On your turn: Fold to give up, Check or Call to stay in, Raise to bet more.
        4. Blinds go in automatically. Waiting? Tick Check / Fold or Call any to act ahead. Everyone gets 30 seconds per move.
        5. Rabbit hunt shows the cards that would have come after everyone folded. Run it twice deals the rest of the board twice when players are all-in, and the pot is split between the two runs. 7-2 bounty: win a hand holding 7-2 and everyone pays you 5 big blinds.
        6. Tap any player to throw a tomato, a beer, a kiss and more. When everyone is all-in the cards flip up and each player's chance of winning is shown. Won without a showdown? Tap Show my cards to brag, or keep them hidden.
        Hand rankings, best to worst
          1. Bet with the chips, then press Deal. You and the dealer get two cards; one dealer card stays face down.
          2. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards are 10, aces are 1 or 11.
          3. Hit takes a card, Stand stops, Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card, Split turns a pair into two hands.
          4. Blackjack (ace + 10-value card) pays 3 to 2. The dealer stands on all 17s. Tap 💡 for the textbook move.
          1. Everyone holds 13 tiles. On your turn you draw one, then discard one. Tap a tile to pick it, tap again (or press Discard) to throw it.
          2. Win with 4 sets + 1 pair. A set is a pong (three alike) or a chow (three in a row, same suit). Seven pairs also wins.
          3. When someone discards, you can Pong or Kong it from anyone, or Chow it from the player on your left. If it completes your hand, shout Mahjong!
          4. Fan make your win bigger: flushes, all pungs, dragon pungs, your seat wind, self-drawn and concealed hands. You have 20 seconds per move. Tap 💡 for a suggestion.
          1. Tap two matching tiles to remove them. Flowers match any flower, seasons match any season.
          2. A tile is free when no tile sits on top of it and its left or right side is open. Dimmed tiles are blocked.
          3. Match quickly to build a combo for bonus points. Clear the board to win coins.
          4. Stuck? Hint shows a pair, Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles into a solvable layout, Undo takes back a match.
